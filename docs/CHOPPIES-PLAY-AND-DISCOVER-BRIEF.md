# Choppies Play & Discover — Product and audience brief

**Status:** Evidence-informed direction, not a claim of Choppies approval or completed user research.  
**Audience:** Choppies shoppers and families in Botswana, with mobile phones as the primary access device.

## Product definition
Choppies Play & Discover is a branded interactive destination—a digital extension of the feeling of discovering something enjoyable around an everyday shopping trip. It is a platform for multiple short experiences, not a single “Choppies game” and not a technical emulator.

The intended journey is: **Recognise → Choose → Play → Share the moment → Return by choice.** The opening should establish identity quickly, the menu must distinguish available experiences from concepts being explored, and games should start quickly with clear controls and forgiving retries.

## Botswana context — evidence
- Statistics Botswana's 2024 household ICT survey reported household internet access at 68.9%, up from 40.6% in 2014; handset mobile broadband was the dominant access method (86.1%). The survey also reported a substantial urban/rural access gap. This supports mobile-first, low-data design and warns against assuming fast connectivity. [Statistics Botswana 2024 ICT survey press release](https://www.statsbots.org.bw/sites/default/files/press-releases/Press%20Release-Statistics%20Botswana%202024%20ICT%20Household%20Survey%20Results.pdf).
- Among households with children under 15 who used the internet, the survey lists YouTube, search, TikTok and online games among common activities. Reported safety incidents include scams, offensive content and bullying. Design safety into the experience; do not add public chat by default. [Statistics Botswana ICT Stats Brief 2024](https://www.statsbots.org.bw/sites/default/files/publications/Botswana%20Household%20Access%20and%20Individual%20Use%20of%20ICT%27s%20Stats%20Brief%202024.pdf).
- Choppies describes its purpose as providing value, growing with communities, and contributing to wellbeing; its mission is to be a one-stop-shop community hub. Its values connect to Botho, including care, respect, honesty, community partnership and innovation. The tone should feel welcoming, playful, practical and community-aware—not luxury-exclusive or pressure-driven. [Choppies Botswana — About](https://choppies.co.bw/about-us/) · [Choppies Group — strategy](https://www.choppiesgroup.com/strategy.php).

## Digital play and psychology — design implications
UNICEF's Responsible Innovation in Technology for Children (RITEC) research describes potential wellbeing benefits from digital play, including agency, creativity, connection and achievement; outcomes depend on design and context. The RITEC toolbox translates research into practical design guidance. [UNICEF RITEC](https://www.unicef.org/innovation/responsible-innovation-technology-children-ritec) · [RITEC Design Toolbox](https://www.unicef.org/childrightsandbusiness/workstreams/responsible-technology/online-gaming/ritec-design-toolbox) · [UNICEF research report, 2024](https://www.unicef.org/innocenti/reports/responsible-innovation-technology-children).

Design rules:
- **Agency:** clear choices, understandable rules, optional help, no forced sign-up before play.
- **Mastery:** short rounds, fair feedback, adjustable challenge, and retries. Reward learning/cooperation, not only speed or winning.
- **Connection:** support family conversation, pass-and-play, or shared goals where feasible; do not assume children play alone.
- **Safety by design:** no public chat, direct messaging, location sharing, public profiles, or child-targeted advertising in the initial experience.
- **Respectful retention:** no punitive streak loss, artificial scarcity, dark patterns, or repeated nagging to install/return.
- **Inclusive access:** readable UI, comfortable touch targets, reduced-motion consideration, English with Setswana localization to evaluate, and low-data asset delivery.
- **Privacy:** collect minimum information; do not collect children's personal information for basic play.

These principles are informed by research, not locally validated findings about every Choppies customer.

## Brand direction
- **Name:** Choppies Play & Discover.
- **Promise:** a little fun for everyone.
- **Personality:** warm, energetic, familiar, curious, fair, community-minded.
- **Visual system:** Choppies red, white for clarity, warm yellow accent, bold friendly typography. Use approved corporate brand assets only when available and permitted.
- **Avoid:** inherited Admin Hub Games purple/teal identity, generic platform jargon, childish clichés, unlicensed supermarket logos, fake offers/prices, or claims of official launch/endorsement.

## Open product questions
1. Which age groups and family compositions should the first activities prioritise? Validate with the product owner and, where possible, real shoppers/caregivers.
2. Which languages should be offered at launch (English and Setswana are candidates)?
3. Will Choppies provide approved brand guidelines, product photography, promotions or sponsorship rules?
4. Is this an independent concept/prototype or an approved customer-facing Choppies initiative? Public claims must reflect confirmed status.
5. What device and connectivity range defines the minimum supported experience?

## Shell acceptance criteria
- Opening presents Choppies Play & Discover identity, then the menu.
- Menu distinguishes real playable inherited prototypes from activities under exploration.
- No activity is described as Choppies-specific or finished unless implemented and verified.
- Website remains usable without installation; native installation remains user-initiated.
- Source archives are not copied to the production public directory.
- No new analytics, accounts, child-data collection, public chat, or backend integration is added by this identity checkpoint.
